Headline of the day

State Dept. notice: USCIS to revise how visa availability is set for adjustment of status

The Visa Bulletin page carries the notice, but the November bulletin is still “Coming Soon”; nothing new in the Federal Register, FLAG or BIA since yesterday.

The official Visa Bulletin page says USCIS, together with the Department of State, is revising how it determines visa availability for applicants waiting to file for employment-based or family-sponsored adjustment of status, aligning it more closely with the consular immigrant visa process. The page gives no effective date or details, and we could not confirm the text of the USCIS announcement today.

In practice: before filing an I-485 based on the “Dates for Filing” chart, check the notice and the USCIS charts page on the day of filing, and avoid promising clients a filing window until the November bulletin is out.
